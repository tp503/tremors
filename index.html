      How to play

      This is a solo-friendly table of the cooperative board game sketched in the design conversation. You control Val, Earl, Rhonda and Burt. If anyone dies, everyone loses.

      • Each character gets 2 actions (1 if Injured or Grabbed). Move, search, work objectives, divert, rescue, fight, use items, rig combos.
      • Every action is at least 1 Noise unless Rhonda has not moved this turn (Don't Move) or you spend a Quiet Move token.
      • Round total: Quiet ≤8, Disturbed 9–14, Frenzy 15+. Location noise decays by 1 at the end of each round.
      • Underground tokens climb a Hunt track. At Hunt 2 they surface and chase the nearest character.
      • First catch by a worm: Threatened. The same worm catching you again: Grabbed. Miss 2 rounds and it submerges at Hunt 1.
      • Grabbed keeps 1 action (Struggle). Rescue or Struggle pulls one node clear. 3 Rescue tokens for the game.
      • Search: d6 + Search + node.search ≥ 9 (the target rises by 1 after each find at that node). Draws from a 16-card item deck into a shared supply.
      • Items are minor alone. Combine two: Pipe Bomb, Tripwire Bait (next surface at a node you pick, no ambush), Fire Bomb, Line Rescue, Field Generator.
      • Evacuate only from round 10+, essentials done, nobody grabbed or dead. Calamities on rounds 4, 7, 10, 12, 14.
      • Finish every Essential objective and Evacuate, or still be alive with them done when round 14 ends.
