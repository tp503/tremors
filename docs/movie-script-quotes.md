# Tremors (1990) — screenplay quotes reference

Source: **Beneath Perfection**, screenplay by S. S. Wilson & Brent Maddock (registered WGAW #379594, draft dated **6/21/88**). Uploaded as `tremors-1990_44fc.pdf`.

Use these for character voice, objective flavour, and calamity text. Keep in-game snippets **short** (one sentence where possible). This draft sometimes spells names and places differently from the finished film (Pham Van, Beneath Perfection, etc.).

## Supporting cast (flavour / calamities)

### Nancy Sterngood & Mindy

- "Mindy, honey, don't pogo in the street."
- "Hey, Mindy, what's the count?" / "Six hundred three, six hundred four…"
- "Burt! Those animals would have killed you!" (normally quiet Nancy)

### Melvin

- "Melvin, touch that truck and die."
- "Oh, wow, no way! No fucking way…" (storage shed)
- "Way to go! Dumb fucking worms! Now haul ass over here… get me off of here…!"

### Pham Van (Walter Chang)

- "Can't reach outside the valley. You know, because of the mountains."
- "Graboids" is **not** in this draft — creatures are "snake things," "worm," "monster," etc.

### Nestor

- "Why not just take a Number Ten pick axe and give it one good whack…?"
- "We've gotta get the police up here. You guys gotta step on it to Bixby."

### Heather Gummer

- "I'd go for penetration. The 458 shooting solids — less ammo to carry anyway."
- "Make noise. Everybody! Come on!"

### Old Fred / Edgar (calamity flavour)

- Edgar on the tower: found dead, rifle in hand, one shoe missing.
- Old Fred's flock: "What the hell…?" — only legs and heads left in a churned pen.
