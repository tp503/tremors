Tremors: Perfection — Playtest Rules

Phase 1 · town only · one death ends it

Co-op, solo-friendly. Run Val → Earl → Rhonda → Burt. Complete the drawn Essentials, then Evacuate from round 8+ (or still have them done when round 12 ends). Anyone Dragged or bled out from Critical = table loss. Prototype, not a published game. Pair with the printable map.

Setup

12 rounds. Calamity on 4 / 7 / 9 / 11 / 12. Start with 3 Rescue, 2 Medkits. Draw 3 Essential, 2 Optional, 1 Character objective. Burt drafts 2 of 3 kit cards. Three Graboids start underground at Hunt 1 in different sectors. Starts: Val & Earl at Chang’s, Rhonda at her camp, Burt at the compound.

Turn

Each character: 2 actions (1 if Injured, Grabbed, or Pinned; 0 if Critical). Grabbed’s action is Struggle. Pinned characters cannot move until helped up.

Every action is at least 1 Noise unless Rhonda took no Move this turn (Don’t Move) or you spend a Quiet Move token (0 Noise).

End of round

  1. Sum Noise this round → Graboid response. Surfaced worms chase the nearest character (1 step; Frenzy/Stampede: 2).
  2. If the round is 4, 7, 9, 11, or 12: draw a Calamity. Band by that same total: Quiet 0–8 / Disturbed 9–14 / Frenzy 15+.
  3. Critical timers tick (2 rounds → dead). Location noise decays by 1. Round noise resets. A surfaced Graboid that catches nobody for 2 rounds submerges at Hunt 1.

Noise & Hunt

Noise is shared and also sits on the node where it happened. After characters act:

Round totalResponse
0–8 QuietNo extra move. Hunt still ticks.
9–11 MovementUnderground worms shift toward prey / loudest noise.
12–14 HuntingMove, and +1 Hunt.
15–19 Feeding FrenzyActivate twice (or extra Hunt).
20+ StampedeA new Graboid enters; all hunt hard.

Each Graboid phase, an underground worm in a sector gains Hunt from that sector’s lingering noise: +1 (0–4), +2 (5–7), +3 (8+), plus Aggression. Hunt 2 = Surface at the loudest node in that sector (ties random). Tripwire Bait overrides the node and skips the ambush.

Kill a surfaced Graboid with 2 wounds → immediately +6 Noise.

The model

Token = sector (underground). Model = node (surfaced). Sharing its node:

  1. Threatened — first catch by that worm. Still mobile. Leave.
  2. Grabbed — the same worm again. 1 action (Struggle). Rescue/Struggle pulls one node clear and breaks the grip.
  3. Dragged — catch while Grabbed. Dead. Everyone loses.

If it attacks (you walk onto it, miss a Fight, or it bursts under you and buildings/towers don’t save you), d6:

d6Result
1–2Miss — Shaken.
3Knockdown — Injured, Shaken.
4Pinned — Injured; cannot move until helped.
5–6Grabbed (if already Threatened by it: this is the second catch).

Already Grabbed + catch = Dragged, no roll. Burt may burn one equipment once per round to cancel an attack at his node (Prepared for Anything).

Health: Healthy → Injured → Critical (2 rounds) → Dead. Val goes Critical after the second serious hit. Shaken: +1 Noise on ordinary Move/Run (not careful). Medkit at the node: Crit→Injured or Injured→Healthy.

Terrain

Building — first Graboid test Compromises it (holds once); later Unsafe. Tower — cannot be grabbed; open ground below becomes Unsafe. Metal — louder (printed N). Open / road — exposed. Solid Rock (Water Tower footing + Caterpillar pad) — revealed by Find the Safe Ground; no surface/catch there. Blocked routes cannot be walked.

Calamity

30 cards, three acts, not fully shuffled: Act I on 4, Act II on 7 and 9, Act III on 11, Get Out of Perfection on 12. The round’s noise band picks Quiet / Disturbed / Frenzy text. Edgar and Old Fred are flavour, not map spaces. Round 12 unfinished Essentials can end the game.
