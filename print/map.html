Phase 1 · the town of Perfection

Perfection, Nevada — Main Street

Lots follow CaciqueCaribe’s 2016 Perfection map: Main St north–south, Talcum Ln across, Chang’s west of the street. Burt, Rhonda, and Jim sit off the town lots. The valley is Phase 2.

What this is

Fourteen nodes. Town lots A–L are the 1990 set. Burt’s hill, Rhonda’s camp, and Jim & Megan’s house are walkable satellites the town map omits. Edgar and Old Fred arrive as calamity news.

How to read a node

Name on top. Under the circle: sector · type · Search rating. Graboids hunt by sector and surface on the loudest node in that sector.
