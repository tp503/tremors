What this is

One node-and-route map. Town buildings sit in the middle. Film valley landmarks (Edgar’s pylon, Old Fred’s, the aqueduct, the Cat, the Bixby Road, the north cliffs) are compressed onto the same graph so the simulated balance is unchanged.
